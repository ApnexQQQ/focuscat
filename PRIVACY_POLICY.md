# Privacy Policy for FocusCat

**Last Updated:** May 6, 2026

## Overview
FocusCat is a parental control and productivity app designed to help children stay focused by blocking distracting apps during study or focus time.

## Camera Usage

### Why We Use the Camera
FocusCat uses the device's front camera **only** for the optional "Reaction Recording" feature:

- **Purpose:** When enabled by parents, the app records a short 10-second video of the child's reaction when they attempt to open a blocked app
- **Use Case:** Parents can view these lighthearted reactions to see their child's response to the focus block
- **Consent:** This feature is **opt-in only** and must be explicitly enabled in Settings
- **Visibility:** A small camera preview (100x100px) is always visible during recording so the child knows they are being recorded

### Camera Data Handling
- Videos are stored **locally** on the device only
- No video data is uploaded to any server or cloud service
- Parents have full control - they can view and delete recordings anytime
- Videos are saved to the app's private storage directory

### Permissions Required
- **CAMERA:** To record video reactions
- **RECORD_AUDIO:** To capture audio with the video

## Overlay Permission

### Why We Use Overlay
FocusCat uses the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (overlay) permission to display a blocking screen when children try to open distracting apps during focus time.

### How It Works
- The overlay appears only during active focus sessions
- It blocks access to configured apps (Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
- The overlay is dismissible by the child to return to their home screen
- Parents can set a PIN to prevent the child from disabling the app

## Data Collection

### What We Collect
FocusCat does **NOT** collect any personal data. All data stays on the device:

- Focus session statistics (stored locally)
- Parent video messages (stored locally)
- Reaction videos (stored locally, only if feature enabled)
- App usage data for blocked apps (stored locally)

### What We DON'T Collect
- No internet connection required for core features
- No account creation needed
- No data sent to external servers
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising

## Data Deletion

Users have full control over their data:
- **Reaction videos:** Can be deleted individually via the Reactions screen
- **All app data:** Can be cleared via Android Settings → Apps → FocusCat → Clear Data
- **Complete removal:** Uninstalling the app removes all data permanently

## Children's Privacy

FocusCat is designed to be used by parents to help their children. The app:
- Does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent
- All features are controlled by the parent device owner
- No data is shared with third parties
