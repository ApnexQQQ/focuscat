# Demo Video Script for Google Play Store

**Purpose:** Show Google reviewers exactly how the app works

**Duration:** 1-2 minutes

**Device:** Samsung S24 Ultra (or any Android phone)

---

## Recording Steps

### 1. App Launch & Setup (15 seconds)
- Open FocusCat app
- Show main screen with timer
- Tap "Start Focus" for 5 minutes
- Show countdown starting

### 2. Block Feature Demo (30 seconds)
- Exit to home screen
- Open Instagram (or TikTok)
- **Show the cat overlay appearing**
- Show the message "Hey... you said you'll focus"
- Tap "Continue Focus" to dismiss
- Show you're back at home screen

### 3. Reaction Recording Feature (45 seconds)
- Go back to FocusCat
- Open Settings (gear icon)
- Tap "Enable Reaction Recording"
- Grant camera permission if prompted
- Go back to main screen
- Open Instagram again
- **Show the small camera preview appearing in corner**
- Wait 10 seconds for recording to complete
- Show "Reaction saved!" toast
- Tap "Reactions" button
- Show the recorded video playing

### 4. Parent Features (20 seconds)
- Show Settings menu
- Show "Record Parent Message" option
- Show "Change PIN" option
- Show stats/streak display

### 5. End (5 seconds)
- Show "FocusCat - Help your kids stay focused"
- End screen

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Use screen recorder** built into Samsung (swipe down → Screen Recorder)
2. **Enable microphone** so you can narrate
3. **Do it in one take** - no editing needed
4. **Speak clearly** explaining each feature
5. **Show the camera preview clearly** - this is what Google wants to see

---

## What Google Wants to See

✅ Camera preview is VISIBLE during recording
✅ Recording is OPT-IN (disabled by default)
✅ Child can dismiss overlay easily
✅ No hidden/secret recording
✅ Parent has full control

---

## Upload to YouTube (Unlisted)

1. Upload video to YouTube
2. Set to "Unlisted"
3. Copy the link
4. Paste in Google Play Console "App Access" section

This dramatically speeds up Google Play review for sensitive permission apps.
