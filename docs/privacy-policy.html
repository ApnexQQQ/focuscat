FocusCat Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Overview

FocusCat is a parental control app designed to help parents manage their children's screen time. We take privacy seriously, especially when it comes to children.

Data We Collect

Camera & Video: The app can record short videos of your child's reaction when blocked apps are accessed. These videos are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

Usage Data: We track which apps are blocked and for how long. This data stays on your device.

No Internet Data Collection: FocusCat does not send any data to external servers. Everything stays on your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: To record optional reaction videos
  • Microphone: To record audio with reaction videos
  • Storage: To save videos and app settings
  • Accessibility: To detect when blocked apps are opened
  • Overlay: To display the blocking screen

Third Parties

We do not share data with any third parties. We do not use analytics, advertising, or cloud services.
