🔒 FocusCat Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 6, 2026

Quick Summary: FocusCat is a parental control app that helps children stay focused. We collect ZERO personal data. Everything stays on your device. No servers, no tracking, no ads.

📸 Camera Usage

Why We Use the Camera

FocusCat uses the front camera only for the optional "Reaction Recording" feature:

  • Purpose: Records a short 10-second video when a child attempts to open a blocked app
  • Use Case: Parents can view these lighthearted reactions
  • Consent: This feature is opt-in only and disabled by default
  • Visibility: A small camera preview is always visible during recording

Camera Data Handling

  • ✅ Videos stored locally on device only
  • ✅ No upload to any server or cloud
  • ✅ Parents have full control - view and delete anytime
  • ✅ Saved to app's private storage directory

Permissions Required

  • CAMERA: To record video reactions
  • RECORD_AUDIO: To capture audio with video

🔲 Overlay Permission

FocusCat uses SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission to display a blocking screen when children try to open distracting apps during focus time.

How It Works

  • Overlay appears only during active focus sessions
  • Blocks access to configured apps (Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
  • Child can dismiss to return to home screen
  • Parents can set PIN to prevent disabling

📊 Data Collection

What We Collect

FocusCat does NOT collect any personal data. All data stays on the device:

  • Focus session statistics (local only)
  • Parent video messages (local only)
  • Reaction videos (local only, if enabled)
  • App usage data for blocked apps (local only)

What We DON'T Collect

  • ❌ No internet connection required
  • ❌ No account creation
  • ❌ No data sent to servers
  • ❌ No analytics or tracking
  • ❌ No advertising

👶 Children's Privacy

FocusCat is designed for parents to help their children:

  • No data collection from children under 13 without parental consent
  • All features controlled by parent device owner
  • No data shared with third parties

🗑️ Data Deletion

Users have full control:

  • Reaction videos: Delete individually via Reactions screen
  • All app data: Clear via Android Settings → Apps → FocusCat → Clear Data
  • Complete removal: Uninstall app to remove all data permanently
